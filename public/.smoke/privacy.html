CODING SESSIONS PORTAL

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

The short version. We store the minimum needed to run a coding sessions portal: who you are, what you have attended, and what you have chosen to display. There is no advertising, no analytics, no tracking, and no selling or sharing of your data with anyone for marketing. You can delete everything at any time from Settings > Delete account, and it is removed immediately.

1. Who runs this portal

The Coding Sessions Portal is run by Ethan Jones. For anything about this policy, or to ask what we hold about you, contact ethan@garyjones.co.uk.

2. How you sign in

Signing in uses Google OAuth. We never see or store your Google password. Google sends us your account identifier, your email address, your name and a link to your Google profile picture. We store your Google account identifier and email address so that signing in again recognises you.

3. What we store, and why

DataWhy
Google account identifier and email address To recognise you when you sign in.
Your name Shown on attendance registers and session lists so other members know who attended.
A profile picture you upload Shown next to your name. Optional; you can remove it at any time.
Your attendance Whether you were present, absent, late or excused for each session.
Group membership Which groups you belong to, set by an administrator.
A reason, if your account is suspended So we can tell you why, rather than leaving you guessing.
Sign-in and activity records An administrative audit trail, used to investigate problems and misuse. Records of a deleted account keep the action but drop your identity.

4. Cookies

Four cookies are set. All are essential to signing in safely and none are used for tracking or advertising.

CookiePurpose
csp_sessionKeeps you signed in. HttpOnly, so scripts cannot read it.
csp_csrfProves a change came from this site and not another.
csp_oauth_browserShort-lived, used only while signing in with Google.
csp_blockedVery short-lived, used once to explain a sign-in refusal.

5. Who can see what

  • Other members see your display name, your profile picture if you have one, and your attendance. They do not see your email address.
  • Administrators see your email address. In the account list it is masked until they choose to reveal it, and that choice is remembered on their browser only.
  • Nobody signed out can see anything. Every page that shows a picture or a record requires a valid sign-in.
  • Sessions can be restricted to particular people or groups. A session you are not on does not appear for you at all.

6. Achievements

If achievements are enabled, what you have earned may be shown to you and to other members. Each achievement can be set to show to everyone, to chosen groups only, or to particular people, and any of those choices can be withdrawn at any time by an administrator. Your achievements are part of your account and are deleted with it.

7. What we do not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising networks.
  • No analytics, tracking pixels or third-party tracking scripts of any kind.
  • No selling, renting or trading of your data.
  • No sharing of your data with anyone for marketing.
  • No third-party embeds, chat widgets or social media feeds on the portal.

The only third parties involved at all are Google, for sign-in, and Cloudflare, which hosts the site and stores the database on our behalf. Your Google profile picture is never fetched from Google: if you upload a picture, it is stored in our own database.

8. How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your account, sign-in identity, stored picture, attendance and group memberships are removed. Audit records keep the fact that an action happened but no longer identify you. Coding sessions you created stay on the portal with your name removed.

9. Your rights

You can, from your own account, without asking anyone:

  • see what we hold, on your profile page;
  • change your display name;
  • upload or remove your profile picture;
  • delete your account and everything in it, permanently.

Deletion is immediate and there is no recovery. We cannot restore an account once it is gone, so please be certain before you confirm.

10. Security

Passwords are never stored, because there are none. Sign-in tokens are random, stored only as hashes, marked HttpOnly, Secure and SameSite=Lax. Every state-changing request is checked against a cross-site request forgery token. Uploads are accepted only if their actual file signature is a PNG, JPEG, WebP or GIF, so a file that merely claims to be an image is rejected. Everything is sent over HTTPS.

11. Children

This portal is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has an account here, contact us and it will be removed.

12. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that affects what we do with your data, the date at the top of this page will change. Significant changes will be announced on the portal.
